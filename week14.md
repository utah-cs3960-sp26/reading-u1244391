Scenario testing is good at catching bugs that show up when the whole system is working together, like broken user flows, integration issues, or cases where different parts of the app do not interact correctly. It is less effective for small detailed bugs inside individual functions or rare edge cases that are not included in the scenarios, so it can miss problems if the test coverage is too limited. It makes the most sense to use it for checking real world behavior of complex systems where you care about end to end results rather than small pieces. Mocks in unit tests are simpler because they fake specific parts of a system to make testing easier and more controlled, while the Digital Twin Universe is like a much larger version of that idea that simulates whole external services so you can test full workflows without using the real systems. They are similar in purpose since both help with testing, but they operate at different scales and you could realistically use both together, with mocks for small unit tests and digital twins for larger scenario based testing.
